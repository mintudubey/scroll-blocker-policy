Privacy Policy & Child Safety Standards

App Name: Scroll Blocker

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

Scroll Blocker is committed to protecting user privacy. Our application is designed as a productivity and digital wellbeing utility tool to help users manage screen time and curb addictive short-form video consumption (such as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts). We believe in complete data privacy: we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

2. Use of Accessibility API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Scroll Blocker uses Android's AccessibilityService API strictly for core app functionality. Specifically, it is used to:

  • Detect when a short-form video feed (e.g., Reels or Shorts) is active in the foreground.
  • Enforce user-configured screen limits and overlays to block access to these specific features.

Data Handling Notice: The Accessibility Service operates completely locally on your device. Scroll Blocker does NOT track, read, record, or transmit typed text, personal messages, audio, video, or browsing history.

3. Additional Permissions

  • Display Over Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to display lock screens, break countdowns, and puzzle barriers over restricted apps when limits are reached.

4. Child Safety Standards (CSAE & CSAM Policy)

Scroll Blocker enforces strict compliance with Google Play Child Safety Policies regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE):

  • Zero Tolerance: Scroll Blocker maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards CSAM and CSAE.
  • No User-Generated Content: Scroll Blocker is a local system utility app. It does not provide chat messaging, content posting, social feeds, image/video sharing, or direct interaction between users.
  • Compliance & Reporting: We comply with all regional and national child protection laws and fully cooperate with law enforcement authorities in reporting any illegal activities or concerns.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Scroll Blocker operates 100% offline for its core features. It does not utilize third-party tracking, profiling analytics, or hidden data mining SDKs.

6. Parental Control & Anti-Uninstall Security

Scroll Blocker provides optional PIN protection and uninstall prevention options to assist parents in setting limits for children. These options are configured locally by the primary device owner.
